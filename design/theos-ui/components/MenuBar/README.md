The in-window menu bar (Windows and Linux) and its dropdowns. Menu labels are Instrument, 10px uppercase, `textDim`, `text` when hovered or open. Items are sentence case at 12px.

**Use**
```html
<nav class="tu-menubar is-keys" role="menubar" aria-label="Main menu">
  <span class="tu-menubar__slot">
    <button class="tu-menubar__item is-open" role="menuitem" aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded="true"><span class="tu-menubar__key">F</span>ile</button>
    <div class="tu-menu">
     <div class="tu-menu__list" role="menu" aria-label="File">
      <div class="tu-menu__item is-active" role="menuitem"><span class="tu-menu__glyph"></span><span class="tu-menu__label">Open project…</span><span class="tu-menu__keys">Ctrl+O</span></div>
      <div class="tu-menu__sep" role="separator"></div>
      <div class="tu-menu__item is-disabled" role="menuitem" aria-disabled="true"><span class="tu-menu__glyph"></span><span class="tu-menu__label">Save<span class="tu-menu__phase">arrives with P12</span></span><span class="tu-menu__keys">Ctrl+S</span></div>
     </div>
    </div>
  </span>
</nav>
```
- **States.** `tu-menubar__item`: `is-open` (`panel2` with a `borderHi` edge on three sides) and `is-focused` (amber outline, as the focus ring of every control). `tu-menubar.is-keys` underlines the access letters (`tu-menubar__key`) while the keyboard drives the bar. `tu-menu__item`: `is-active` (the highlighted row: 13% amber wash and a 2px amber edge, as `tu-pop__row`; its keys turn `text`), `is-current` (the current layout preset or Codex-tree tab: its glyph, ▸ for a tab, is amber; it is also `aria-checked="true"`), `is-disabled` (`textMute`, not highlighted on hover; focusable but it does nothing, see Keyboard model).
- **Parts.** `tu-menu__glyph` (22px gutter), `tu-menu__label`, `tu-menu__phase` ("arrives with PNN", mono `textDim`, inside the label), `tu-menu__keys` (mono, right-aligned), `tu-menu__more` (the `▸` of a submenu), `tu-menu__sep`, `tu-menu__group` (small uppercase heading). A submenu is a `tu-menu tu-menu__sub` popup opening to the right of its row. It is a sibling of the parent's `tu-menu__list`, inside the parent `tu-menu`, never a descendant of the scrolling list (the list's `overflow-y` would clip it). `role="menu"` is on each `tu-menu__list`, not on the `tu-menu` popup, so a submenu is never a menu inside a menu. The parent row carries `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` (the submenu list's `id`); `--sub-top` on the submenu is the row's offset from the top of the parent menu, minus 5px (the submenu's own border and padding), so the build sets it from the row's `offsetTop` minus the list's `scrollTop`. The View menu's Layout submenu holds the layout presets as `role="menuitemradio"` with `aria-checked` (the Codex-tree tabs likewise); other menu rows are `role="menuitem"`.
- **Scrolling.** `tu-menu__list` holds the rows and scrolls on its own (`max-height`: 100vh minus `h-titlebar` and the border, `overflow-y: auto`, themed scrollbar), so a menu taller than the window scrolls inside the window and the page never does. A submenu's list ends inside the window: its `max-height` also subtracts `--sub-top`, and when its rows do not fit, its list scrolls. A submenu opens beside its row to the right, and the build adds `is-left` to open it to the left of its menu when it would cross the window's right edge. Vertically there is no shift or flip: it does not move up to fit, so near the bottom of the window its list gets shorter and scrolls. `tu-menu--static` draws a menu flat in a page, its list without a limit.
- **Sizes.** A dropdown is as wide as its widest row, at least `w-menu-min` (248px) and at most `w-menu-max` (420px); a label that still does not fit is cut with an ellipsis (its "arrives with PNN" goes first) and never runs under the keys column; a row is `h-menu-item` (24px). The menu is `panel2` with a `borderHi` edge, `shadow-popover`, radius 0.
- **Keyboard model.** Alt pressed and released alone, or F10, focuses the first menu (`is-focused`, `is-keys`). Left and Right move between menus; Down, Enter and Space open the focused menu; a click on a label opens its menu (a second click closes it); a letter opens the menu with that access letter; Esc closes the menu, then returns focus to where it was. Alt with an access letter opens that menu directly. In an open menu Up and Down move the highlighted row; disabled rows stay focusable (WAI-ARIA menu pattern) and Enter on one does nothing; a letter jumps to the row whose label starts with it, and Enter runs the row (a click runs it too); Right opens a submenu, Left closes it. Hovering another label while a menu is open switches to it. One menu is open at a time.
- **Closing.** Tauri's drag script calls `preventDefault` and `stopImmediatePropagation` on `mousedown` inside a drag region, so a `mousedown` listener on `document` never sees a click on the breadcrumb. Close an open menu on a `pointerdown` listener on `window` in the capture phase (a press outside the menu), and on window blur.
- **Content.** Rows come from the command table (labels, keys and enabled state are not typed into the design); a disabled row names its phase.
- Contrast: label, item and key text pass 4.5:1 in both themes; disabled items are `textMute` by design.
