Listeners are ancestors who answer to the right song

codex/beliefs/b-020.md3 links in · 1 lensedited 2h ago

The binders treat every Listener as an ancestor: when one answers a song, they hear a grandmother recognising her own name. The belief distorts T-012Listeners are the unwoven Tuners, and it survives because it predicts the right behaviour.

Design note: the player first meets this belief at the Fates' shrine on F4, before any clue to T-012 is confirmed.
