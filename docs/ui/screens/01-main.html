Floor 4 — The Choir-hall

world/hearth/f04-choir-hall/floor.md12 links in · 2 lenses · 2 conceptsedited 20 min ago · Jann

A grassland floor grown over a half-buried Choir-hall nave. The Hearth folk have farmed its slopes for generations and built the Fates' shrine on the roof of the ruin, where the pipes still hum at dusk. It is the first floor where the Tower answers back.

What the player should leave with

  • The hum is not weather. Something in the hall responds to song.
  • The Hearth folk have a complete, comforting explanation for it, and it is wrong.
  • One word in a dead tongue, heard once under the shrine: C-031the murmur.
