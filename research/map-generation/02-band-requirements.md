# 02 · Band requirements

Stage 1 of the map generation research. What each band needs from a map, in generator terms.

Status: confirmed by Jann on 11 Nov 2026 with the changes in `decisions.md` (giant trees are
climbed terrain, type I; band 4 runs from cavern to labyrinth; bands blend). Sources: the
ten-bands decision (themes fixed for now) and the Tower proposal (what a floor is, by act).
Everything in the columns "Terrain types", "Scale" and "Sample floors" is derived for this
research and is not canon.

## Terrain types

The bands need nine kinds of terrain. Later stages research each kind once.

| Id | Terrain type | Short description | Bands |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | River landscape | Valleys, hills, mountains, lakes shaped by rivers | 1, 2, 3; base of 5 |
| B | Needle peaks | Steep rock towers standing in a void or cloud sea | 5 |
| C | Cavern network | Caves, tunnels and labyrinths without a sky | 4 |
| D | Town and castle | Pre-industrial settlements with walls | 1, 2, 3, 5 |
| E | City | A built-up floor: vertical brass city, modern street grid, ruined city | 6, 7, 8 |
| F | Broken ground | Floating blocks, gaps, drifting islands | 8; rims of 6 and 7 |
| G | Machine hall | Very large interiors on several levels | 9; ruins in every band |
| H | Ring gallery | Walkways and platforms around the open Bore | 10 |
| I | Giant trees | Kilometre-tall trees the player climbs (added at checkpoint 1) | 3 |

## Gameplay needs per band

| Band | Hook (from the bands decision) | What a map must provide |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tutorial floors, short safe runs, first Chimes | Short walks between Chimes; a hub village; few threats on the way |
| 2 | Sieges and castle crawls; floor 20 closes the act | A castle as a child map with an interior; a siege approach |
| 3 | Large open hunts, spirit binding | Long distances; lairs and groves as places; few roads |
| 4 | Tight, dark dungeon runs; echo-vaults | Room graphs with lock and key; sightlines kept short |
| 5 | Duels and sect trials; floor 50 is the halfway gate | Arenas on summits; routes that are bridges and stairs |
| 6 | Vertical cities, first Tuner technology | Routes across tiers; lifts; districts |
| 7 | Urban combat | Blocks with interiors; cover along streets |
| 8 | Classic extraction terrain | Disconnected pieces with few crossings; clear extraction points |
| 9 | The machine plainly visible | Readable hall layout; operator-plates as places |
| 10 | The final weave | One route up the rings; the conductor's gallery |

Every band also needs, on each floor: a main settlement or safe place with a Chime, more Chimes
out in the land, and the way up (a tuning gallery with its guardian). Every tenth floor has the
band's guardian. Whether every floor's gate has an arena is decision D10 of the synthesis.
